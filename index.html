Biography

I am a 1st-year Ph.D. student at Peking Union Medical College Hospital (PUMCH), Chinese Academy of Medical Sciences (CAMS) & Peking Union Medical College (PUMC) (北京协和医院, 中国医学科学院 & 北京协和医学院(清华大学医学部)), jointly trained with the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CASIA) （中国科学院自动化研究所）, advised by Prof. Ming Feng and Prof. Bo Xu. I am also a research intern at Alibaba DAMO Academy (阿里巴巴达摩院) since 2024, working with Dr. Dakai Jin and Dr. Le Lu (IEEE Fellow). Previously, I received my M.Eng. in Software Engineering from Xi’an Jiaotong University (XJTU) (西安交通大学) in 2025, advised by Prof. Zhongyu Li in the CVML group led by Prof. Jihua Zhu, where I also worked closely with Dr. Xiangde Luo. If you are interested in collaborating with me or want to have a chat, always feel free to contact me through e-mail.
